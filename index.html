Hardware & devices

Firmware for wearable sensors, and a voice assistant I built on a Raspberry Pi.

HAL — voice assistant

A home-built voice assistant on a Raspberry Pi 5, styled after HAL 9000. It listens for a wake word, transcribes speech locally, replies through Claude and starts speaking before the answer is finished. It has an OLED face, an iPhone companion app, long-term memory per person, timers and music, and over 12,000 lines of Python with automated tests.

PythonRaspberry PiWhisperClaude APIPiper TTS

Runs on my home network, so there's no public demo.

Multimodal wearable firmware

Firmware that runs seven sensors at once (optical, heart, motion, CO₂, temperature, particulates and electrochemical) and logs CRC-protected, timestamped data to microSD, then reads it back to verify.

CZephyr / nRF ConnectAD5941

Research code, private.

Potentiostat CV/LSV firmware

Voltammetry firmware for a wearable potentiostat. Includes a live UART plotter and a hardware debugging investigation that traced a 0.22 V peak shift to a constant DAC offset, rather than iR drop.
