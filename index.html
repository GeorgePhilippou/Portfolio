HAL — voice assistant

A home-built voice assistant on a Raspberry Pi 5, in a hand-built black monolith case inspired by 2001: A Space Odyssey, with a HAL-9000-style eye on an OLED screen. It listens for a wake word, transcribes speech locally, replies through Claude and starts speaking before the answer is finished. It has an iPhone companion app, long-term memory per person, timers and music, and over 12,000 lines of Python with automated tests.

PythonRaspberry PiWhisperClaude APIPiper TTS

Runs on my home network, so there's no public demo.
